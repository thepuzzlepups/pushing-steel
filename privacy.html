Privacy policy

Pushing Steel doesn't collect any information about you.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What the app keeps, and where

Your workouts, templates, custom exercises and settings are stored on your devices and synced between them through your own iCloud account (Apple's private iCloud database for you alone). So your Mac can show your weight, the app also keeps a copy of your recent weigh-ins in your iCloud. We can't see any of it.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Pushing Steel saves your workouts and weigh-ins to the Health app and reads your weight, height, heart rate, active energy and Activity rings to show them alongside your training. Health data stays in Health and your iCloud. It is never sent to us, never used for advertising and never shared with anyone.

What we collect

Nothing. Pushing Steel has no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no server. It doesn't use your location, contacts or photos.

Deleting your data

Delete workouts in the app, or delete the app from your devices. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on iPhone. Workouts and weigh-ins saved to Health are deleted in the Health app.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
